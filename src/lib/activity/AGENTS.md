# src/lib/activity

- TSD history batches share omni.server.ts's wallet-path resolver with confirmation reads; failed Omni lookups keep recovery backfills incomplete, and a recovery scan passes known native transactions. Why: wallet-scoped node errors must not permanently hide confirmed TSD receipts or mistake their carrier outputs for TXC deposits.

- Member history = /activity (src/lib/activity/: activity-rules.ts + deposit-rules.ts pure + tested, activity.server.ts reads only the signed-in member's rows, a swap's inner steps never listed twice; one-tap chains (hype_chains) show as one row and every row with a parent_id is hidden as its step). Every swap row links to its own record on /swap through swapHref/parseSwapRef (?sw=kind:id, validated there; MySwaps scrolls to it and opens its Details), so one-line rows are never a dead end. Deposits live in pocket_deposits (server-written, owner read, history rows can't be edited/deleted): chain history for TXC (+Omni TSD), ISK, Ethereum, Base, BNB (listed tokens only); other coins only as balance rises from now on, less Seeds' own deliveries; a network that doesn't answer never records anything. /trades forwards to /activity. Why: one exchange-style history that never shows a coin the member didn't really receive, and every row can be traced to the record behind it.
