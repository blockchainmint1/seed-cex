# PulseChain

- PulseChain withdrawals (PLS, pHEX) follow the same rules as every coin (src/lib/withdraw/AGENTS.md). HEX on Ethereum is "HEX", HEX on PulseChain is "pHEX" (separate legs/markets); their prices come from CoinGecko/DexScreener in cmc.server.ts, never CMC by symbol. Why: HME Wallet can't send on PulseChain, and the two HEX coins differ in price several-fold.
- Withdrawal dollar caps and the hold come from the member's level (src/lib/withdraw-limits.ts pure + tested, withdraw-limits.server.ts reads levels/history/overrides; edited in /admin/settings via save_withdrawal_tiers / set_withdrawal_override, which check admin, refuse self-changes and log to withdrawal_limit_events). Missing levels fail closed; a paused member is blocked at request and again at send. Why: new members start low and earn higher limits from history, and every change is accountable.
- Hyperliquid: HYPE is supported only on HyperEVM (chain "hyperevm", 999, native HYPE, same 1106 EVM pocket address), leg "hype", market HYPE_TSD; Seeds-sent withdrawals with fee cap EVM_MAX_FEE.hyperevm; 30 confirmations; price from CoinGecko id "hyperliquid" (DexScreener WHYPE backup), and seedsPlace links HYPE only when the name is "Hyperliquid". HyperCore (Hyperliquid's own chain) is not supported. Why: copycat HYPE tokens share the ticker, and HyperCore sends need a separate signer.
