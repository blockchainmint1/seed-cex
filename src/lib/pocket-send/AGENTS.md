# Pocket sends

- Every send from an HME pocket (exchange leg or P2P release) lives in src/lib/pocket-send/: rules.ts (pure, tested), pocket.server.ts (read/sign), approvals.server.ts (send_approvals + signed Telegram Approve/Decline), trade.server.ts; a send must spend a live approval matching coin, amount, destination and link, and exchange legs send only from autosettle after trades.settle_started_at is claimed. Exchange legs normally spend an approval derived from the order's own tap (src/lib/order-approval/), not a per-trade tap. Why: no coins leave a member's pocket without their own tap, and two taps can't start two sends.
- Approve-in-HME-Wallet links can't send from Seeds (no key); they stay blocked with a plain reason until HME Wallet has a signing flow. Why: Seeds must never pretend a send happened.
