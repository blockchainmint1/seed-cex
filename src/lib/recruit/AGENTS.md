# Coin recruiting (/admin/marketing, first tab; Ads and Coin videos are the other tabs)
- Rules live only in recruit-rules.ts (pure, tested): stages + allowed moves (paid/live never move back, closed leads only reopen to Found), the 0–100 lead score worked out from CoinMarketCap numbers and contact links, which coins count as a coin team (no tokenized stocks, stablecoins, wrapped coins), duplicate keys (CMC id → contract → ticker+website), pasted-list parsing, the all-to-all pair math for the goal, and outreachProblems (blocks "no listing fee", "bankrupt"/"stole" about BitMart, guarantees, placeholders, over-length). Why: every number an admin sees must be reproducible, and the AI must never set a score or invent a fact.
- recruit.server.ts does the CoinMarketCap reads (listings, exchange market pairs, quotes, info links) and the AI drafting; the AI may use only the PITCH facts and the lead's stored numbers. recruit.functions.ts is admin-gated (requireAdmin on every call); recruit_leads / recruit_messages / recruit_events are service-role only. Why: lead lists and outreach are private business data.
- Seeds never sends outreach: an admin copies the message, sends it from their own account, then presses "I sent it", which re-runs outreachProblems server-side, moves Found/Shortlisted to Contacted, and logs the event. AI drafts are capped per day (AI_DRAFTS_PER_DAY). Why: Bobby wants a person to approve every message, and cold-sending from Seeds' email would damage its sender reputation.
