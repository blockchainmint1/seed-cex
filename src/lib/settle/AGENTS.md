# src/lib/settle

- Exchange settlement is two-step (src/lib/settle/: confirm-rules.ts pure + tested, confirm.server.ts run every minute by the send-approvals job): autosettle reserves both sides' approvals (derived from each order's placement tap, src/lib/order-approval/), sends the taker's side only; the maker's side goes out after the first is confirmed to CONFIRMATIONS_NEEDED; a trade is settled only when both escrows have confirmed_at. A send the network says doesn't exist (after 20 min; TXC/ISK only while getindexinfo shows a synced txindex, a failed check re-asked after 1 min; EVM only when two independent servers say so, lookupVerdict) is first resent once from escrows.sent_raw (EVM: not if the confirmed nonce passed sent_nonce and that same server has no receipt for it; an EVM "nonce too low" re-looks before blaming; unanswered resends retry 5 min apart); still missing, replaced, spent elsewhere or rejected → dispute + profiles.trading_frozen_at for that payer, which also blocks P2P opens/releases (except dispute rulings) and outside-address swaps (admin lifts, logged); our servers not answering or a slow send never blames anyone (6 h → admin). Any broadcast error that may have gone out (broadcast-rules.ts maybeBroadcast, tested) is never re-sent or marked failed. Why: audit #3 A1/D2 — members hold their own keys and could replace a send after Seeds pays the other side.
- Order-book readiness (src/lib/order-readiness.ts, tested): matchOrder refuses an order unless the placer can pay it now (orderPayProblem: pocketSender "trade" + free pocket balance after everything set aside, src/lib/set-asides.ts); after a match is cancelled before anything moved, only the side at fault (payer setup/balance, or receiver with no address) loses its order, a network failure restores both; the public tape/stats skip timed_out/refunded trades. Exchange legs use pocketSender(…, "trade"): Telegram tap required, no two-factor-before-link rule (other pocket sends keep it). Why: dead orders kept the book crossed and matching-then-failing forever, and a cancelled match never traded.
- Auditor order-book review (Nov 22, Bobby approved R1–R4): a trade leg's Telegram tap shows buy/sell, amount, price, what comes back and the recent price (pocket-send/trade-terms.ts, tested), warns above FAR_FROM_MARKET_PCT in the member's bad direction, and the approval stores side + priceKey in meta, which reserve/spend must match; three non-network failed matches on a member's side in 24 hours (cancelled_preflight detail.culprit) pause placing orders until the oldest ages out (order-readiness.ts repeatFailurePause, order-pause.server.ts), admins clear via clearOrderPause (profiles.order_pause_cleared_at); Place order may reuse a ≤15 s shared-cache balance only to accept, never to refuse; the public tape prints only trades with trades.first_sent_at (set by DB triggers when a side is sent or funded). No daily cap for members without two-factor (Bobby: "No limit"). Why: the tap is the only gate on trade sends, repeat griefing must cost something, and cancelled matches must never move the public last price.
- Matched trades whose payout never started (audit O6): the minute job's resumeUnstartedTrades (confirm.server.ts; due rule unstartedTradeDue in confirm-rules.ts, tested) re-runs autoSettleTrade on trades still "matched" with no settle_started_at once they are UNSTARTED_RETRY_AFTER_MS old, claimed by CAS on trades.settle_retry_at; a fill approval derived twice at once reuses the one live row (unique send_approvals_one_live → fillApprovalFromOrder re-reads it). Why: the request is marked placed before settlement, so a cut-off worker must not leave coins "In trades" with nothing retrying.
