# src/lib/settle

- Exchange settlement is two-step (src/lib/settle/: confirm-rules.ts pure + tested, confirm.server.ts run every minute by the send-approvals job): autosettle reserves both sides' Telegram approvals, sends the taker's side only; the maker's side goes out after the first is confirmed to CONFIRMATIONS_NEEDED; a trade is settled only when both escrows have confirmed_at. A send the network says doesn't exist (after 20 min; TXC/ISK only while getindexinfo shows a synced txindex, a failed check re-asked after 1 min; EVM only when two independent servers say so, lookupVerdict) is first resent once from escrows.sent_raw (EVM: not if the confirmed nonce passed sent_nonce and that same server has no receipt for it; an EVM "nonce too low" re-looks before blaming; unanswered resends retry 5 min apart); still missing, replaced, spent elsewhere or rejected → dispute + profiles.trading_frozen_at for that payer, which also blocks P2P opens/releases (except dispute rulings) and outside-address swaps (admin lifts, logged); our servers not answering or a slow send never blames anyone (6 h → admin). Any broadcast error that may have gone out (broadcast-rules.ts maybeBroadcast, tested) is never re-sent or marked failed. Why: audit #3 A1/D2 — members hold their own keys and could replace a send after Seeds pays the other side.
- Order-book readiness (src/lib/order-readiness.ts, tested): matchOrder refuses an order unless the placer can pay it now (orderPayProblem: pocketSender "trade" + free pocket balance after their other open orders); after a match is cancelled before anything moved, only the side at fault (payer setup/balance, or receiver with no address) loses its order, a network failure restores both; the public tape/stats skip timed_out/refunded trades. Exchange legs use pocketSender(…, "trade"): Telegram tap required, no two-factor-before-link rule (other pocket sends keep it). Why: dead orders kept the book crossed and matching-then-failing forever, and a cancelled match never traded.
