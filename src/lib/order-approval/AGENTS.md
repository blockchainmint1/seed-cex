# Order approval (one tap per order)

- The member's Telegram tap happens when an order is PLACED, never after a match (Bobby, Nov 23 2026). placeOrder / API POST /order only create an order_requests row and a send_approvals row (purpose "order", leg = the coin the member pays, amount = orderApprovalCap: sell size, or buy size × limit/protection price); the order is created with the request's id and matched only after Approve (continueOrderRequest). Market orders get 3 minutes to tap and run at the protection price shown in the tap; limit orders get 15 minutes, then the approval lasts until the order fills or is cancelled (ORDER_APPROVAL_LIFETIME_MS backstop; the DB guard lets an order approval set its expiry once, in the approving update). Why: a market order can't wait for a tap after it executed, and a resting order must trade whenever it's hit.
- Each fill's trade_leg approval is derived in autosettle from the payer's order approval (fillApprovalFromOrder, rules in rules.ts, tested): same member, link and pocket address, same coin and side, fill price at the order's limit or better, cumulative fills never above the approved total (cancelled / never-sent fills give it back). A failed check cancels the trade with the payer at fault; an order with no order approval never trades (autosettle calls the match off, nobody blamed), and the minute job cancels any such order with cancel_reason "reapprove_needed" and sends its owner one Telegram note (reapprove.server.ts; Bobby: no old-style taps ever again). Cancelling an order (site, Cancel all, API) ends its approval. Why: the tap stays the only gate on trade sends, but it covers exactly the terms the member saw.
- Approved orders never depend on the tap's own run (audit O1): placeApprovedOrder claims the request under a lease (order_requests.working_until, ORDER_PLACING_LEASE_MS), records "placed" as soon as matching returns, and the minute job (resumeOrderRequests; decisions in orderSweepAction, tested) places approved-but-unplaced requests, finishes ones whose worker died after matching (order row exists → settle, never re-match) and ends ones whose approval ran out with a "price protection ran out" note. Why: a lost webhook must not leave a tapped order in limbo or match it twice.
- When an order approval ends for any reason other than the member's own cancel (cancelApprovals by link on revoke / Telegram change, lifetime), its order comes off the book (cancel_reason "approval_ended") and a bulk end sends one note; a fill refused because the approval ended or the pocket link changed is called off with no culprit (approvalEndedNotMemberFault, tested). Market approvals are never marked used by hand; they run out after ORDER_MARKET_RUN_MS. Why: audit O2/O3 — dead orders must not match and fail with a member strike.
