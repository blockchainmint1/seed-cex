# step-up

- "Confirm it's you" (step-up) lives in src/lib/step-up/ (rules.ts tested, step-up.server.ts, components/account/StepUp.tsx): linking a new coin/wallet pocket, confirming a new 2FA app or Telegram chat, and unlinking or replacing a connected Telegram chat pass only on a sign-in made AFTER the request (stepUpSince: held key claimed_at, factor created_at / chat added_at, or a server-signed 30-min "screen opened at" intent for unlink/replace) and within 10 minutes, or a single-use proof from a day-old trusted Telegram tap, a code from day-old trusted 2FA apps (also after the request), or the Honest.Money email code. No "since" = the sign-in option never passes. Why: auditor H1/H1b/S1 — a stolen tab, even right after login, must not redirect coins or plant its own second factor.
- second_factor_trust (server only) marks each 2FA app/chat. A new one is trusted at once only if it is the first of its kind on the account and the session signed in within 10 minutes (setup guide), or a replacement chat right after a passed step-up; otherwise "Not confirmed yet". Unconfirmed chats can't approve any pocket send or AI buy (pocketSender, requestSendApproval, tap checks). Each item is confirmed on its own (by factor id); unconfirmed 2FA apps can be removed with no check. Why: auditor S2 — confirming your own app must never confirm an attacker's.
